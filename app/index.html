    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, no online trophies, no mission replay, free-roam after the story. PowerPyx rates the run 4 out of 10 and about 200 hours. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them off. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter: there are 15 manual slots and no mission replay.

    The 100% completion checklist, in brief

    It unlocks after the story under Progress, Stats, 100% Completion, and progress made during the story already counts. It is closer to 60% of the game than true 100%: most side quests are not needed and collectibles are only partly required. What needs everything: robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's quests. The partial targets: any 100 optional missions, any 200 of 277 base-model vehicles scanned, any 100 of 255 animals studied, 300 stunts, 15 of 18 meditations, any 70 of 80 challenges. Source: PowerPyx.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy in the whole list is potentially missable. Everything else, story trophies included, can still be earned in free-roam after the story.

    Not missable: the rest of the list

    All 12 story chapter and mission trophies pop automatically and cannot be missed. Optional quest givers who go quiet for a while, such as Kenton, come back later in the story. Time-sensitive quests with an hourglass icon are not needed for any trophy. Some fences and magazine collectibles disappear, but neither affects trophies or 100% completion. Source: PowerPyx.

    Sources and honesty notes

    Official names, descriptions and grades come from the PlayStation trophy list as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles, which agree on every entry. Roadmap facts, counts and methods come from PowerPyx's GTA 6 Trophy Guide and Roadmap, with Push Square and KeenGamer as second sources. Every trophy's guide in this app is written in this entry's own words and names the guide it draws on; no guide text is copied. Two trophies, Bad Luck, I Guess and The Truth Is Out There, were still unconfirmed in the published guides at launch, and their entries say so instead of guessing. This app works fully offline: it fetches only its own data file and makes no network request.
